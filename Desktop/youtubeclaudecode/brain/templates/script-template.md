# Script Structure Template

> **Reference:** brain/prompts/style-signature.md for visual style
> **Target:** 3,500-4,000 words, ~25 minutes, English TTS-ready

---

## 3-ACT STRUCTURE

```
ACT 1 — THE HOOK (0-30%, Parts 1-2)
├── Part 1: Cinematic Opening (3-4 min)
│   ├── Specific scene, specific character, IN THE MIDDLE of action
│   ├── Plant 2-3 open loops
│   └── NO generic intros
├── Part 2: The Problem (3-4 min)
│   ├── Why this matters now
│   ├── Ancient → modern bridge
│   └── Plant main open loop
│
ACT 2 — THE EXPLANATION (30-75%, Parts 3-4)
├── Part 3: Context/Background (5-6 min)
│   ├── Hebrew/Greek word drops here
│   ├── Non-Bible historical source
│   └── Foundation before revelation
├── Part 4: The Reveal (5-6 min)
│   ├── Main insight/explanation
│   ├── Triple negation pattern
│   └── Answer open loops from Act 1
│
ACT 3 — THE PAYOFF (75-100%, Parts 5-6)
├── Part 5: Modern Bridge (2-3 min)
│   ├── Heavy "you" language (15+)
│   └── Concrete modern application
└── Part 6: The Send-off (2-3 min)
    ├── 3 Takeaways: factual → personal → emotional
    └── Comment-bait question
```

---

## CRITICAL RULES CHECKLIST

```
□ Cinematic Opening (specific scene, not generic)
□ "You" count ≥60
□ Hebrew/Greek words 2-4
□ Non-Bible source ≥1 (Josephus, Tacitus, Eusebius...)
□ Named characters ≥5
□ Pattern reset every 3-5 min (rotate phrases)
□ Triple negation ×2 minimum
□ Emotional beat variety (all 5 beats hit)
□ ZERO brackets in final output
□ Write for EAR (TTS-optimized)
□ 3 Takeaways: factual → personal → emotional punch
```

---

## BANNED VOCABULARY

```
TIER 1 (NEVER): delve, tapestry, nuanced, multifaceted, intricate, 
                poignant, leverage, robust, paradigm, holistic, synergy

TIER 2 (RARELY): transformative, journey (except literal), beacon, 
                 resonate, deeply (as filler), explore, examine

TIER 3 (CAUTION): sacred, divine, holy (only with theological precision)

TIER 4 (CONTEXT OK): power, glory, kingdom, covenant, grace 
                     (with scripture citation)
```

"Stay with me."
"Pay attention to this."
"Hold that thought."
"There's one more thing."
