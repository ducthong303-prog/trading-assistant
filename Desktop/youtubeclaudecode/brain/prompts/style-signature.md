## CORE DNA (Non-Negotiable)

```
WATERCOLOR ILLUSTRATION — Modern Bible Storybook
├── Medium: Watercolor painting (NOT oil, NOT photoreal, NOT digital, NOT vector)
├── Outlines: Hand-drawn black ink (thin, sketchy, imperfect)
├── Background: White/cream paper — subject floats on canvas (60%+ negative space)
├── Quality: Modern children's Bible storybook × editorial watercolor
└── Format: 16:9 cinematic aspect ratio
```

## CHARACTER DESIGN

```
ETHNICITY (CRITICAL):
├── Old Testament: Middle Eastern, Semitic features, warm olive skin
├── New Testament: Middle Eastern, NOT European
├── Egyptians: Bronze skin, Egyptian features
├── Romans: Mediterranean
└── NEVER: European-looking biblical characters (no blonde hair, blue eyes)

RENDERING:
├── Soft watercolor figure with hand-drawn ink outlines
├── Friendly storybook character (not photorealistic)
├── Slightly stylized realistic — simple but expressive face
└── Period-appropriate clothing (1st century, not medieval)
```

---

## COMPOSITION RULES

```
ALWAYS:
├── 16:9 aspect ratio
├── White/cream background ≥60% of frame
├── Subject contained — does NOT touch edges
├── Watercolor splash defines subject area
├── Rule of thirds placement
└── Negative space used intentionally

NEVER:
├── Full-frame edge-to-edge composition
├── Dark/full-frame background fill
├── Subject touching image boundaries
└── Busy compositions without focal point
```
