## Deep Made Simple — Gold Standard Analysis

**Why they win:**
- Watercolor style = instantly recognizable brand
- "Every X Explained" title format = high CTR
- Pattern A thumbnails = proven 40% viral rate
- Consistent upload schedule
- Clean audio + visual sync

**What to copy:**
- Watercolor illustration style (adapted, not cloned)
- "Every X Explained" format
- Serif typography (Cinzel/Playfair)
- 3-Act emotional structure

**What to improve:**
- More Hebrew/Greek word drops
- More non-Bible historical sources
- Better A/B testing discipline
- More aggressive pattern variety
