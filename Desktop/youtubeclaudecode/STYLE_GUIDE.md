**Modern Bible storybook watercolor illustration** — hand-painted watercolor with thin black ink outlines, floating on white/cream paper, in a limited warm palette.

## DOs and DON'Ts

### DO
- Watercolor painting aesthetic
- Hand-drawn black ink outlines
- White/cream background (60%+ negative space)
- Middle Eastern accurate characters
- 16:9 aspect ratio
- Subject floats on canvas (doesn't touch edges)

### DON'T
- Oil painting / photorealistic / CGI / 3D
- Dark full-frame backgrounds
- Neon colors / digital gradients
- European biblical characters (blonde, blue eyes)
- Sans-serif fonts (Impact, Bebas Neue, Arial)
- AI text in images (add in Canva)

## Thumbnail Patterns (6 total)

1. **A — Every X Explained** (40% viral): Title + main character/illustration
2. **B — Title Statement** (25%): Big bold statement + visual storytelling
3. **C — Contrast/Comparison** (10%): Split left/right composition
4. **D — Character Grid** (10%): 6-12 portrait grid
5. **E — Quote/Concept Block** (10%): Quote + supporting watercolor scene
6. **F — Icon Row with Labels** (5%): 3-5 icons in progression

## Script Style

- 8th grade clarity, adult emotional depth
- "You" language (60+ instances)
- Cinematic openings (specific scene, not generic)
- Hebrew/Greek drops (2-4 per script)
- Zero brackets in final output
- Write for EAR (TTS), not eye
