## 3-ACT STRUCTURE

### ACT 1 — THE HOOK (Parts 1-2, ~8 min)
**Purpose:** Grab attention, establish stakes, plant open loops.

**Part 1 — Cinematic Opening (3-4 min)**
- Specific scene, specific character, specific moment
- KHÔNG generic intro ("The Bible is a complex book...")
- Start in the MIDDLE of the action
- Plant 2-3 open loops (questions answered later)
- Example: "The spear is still wet. Peter stares at it, breathing hard..."

**Part 2 — The Problem (3-4 min)**
- Why does this matter?
- What's at stake if you don't understand this?
- Bridge ancient context to modern life
- Plant main open loop: "But here's what nobody told you about..."

### ACT 2 — THE EXPLANATION (Parts 3-4, ~12 min)
**Purpose:** Deep dive, deliver on promises, scholarly authority.

**Part 3 — Context/Background (5-6 min)**
- Historical/cultural setup
- Hebrew/Greek word drops here
- Non-Bible historical source (Josephus, Tacitus, Eusebius)
- Build foundation before revelation

**Part 4 — The Reveal (5-6 min)**
- Main explanation/insight
- Answer open loops from Act 1
- Pattern: "This is not A. This is not B. This is not C. This is D."
- Most memorable content here

### ACT 3 — THE PAYOFF (Parts 5-6, ~5 min)
**Purpose:** Emotional resolution, personal application, call to action.

**Part 5 — Modern Bridge (2-3 min)**
- Connect ancient truth to modern life
- "You" language heavy here (15+ instances)
- Concrete, specific application
- KHÔNG generic "apply this to your life"

**Part 6 — The Send-off (2-3 min)**
- 3 Takeaways: factual → personal → emotional punch
- Open loop for next video (optional)
- End strong, not trailing off

## RETENTION MECHANICS

### Pattern Reset Phrases (every 3-5 min)
Rotate through:
- "Here's what that means."
- "Let me show you something."
- "This changes everything."
- "Pay attention to this next part."
- "You need to understand this."

### Triple Negation Pattern
```
This is not [common misconception].
This is not [another wrong view].
This is not [third wrong view].
This is [truth].
```

### Open Loop Economy
- Plant: 3-4 open loops in Act 1
- Answer: 2-3 in Act 2, 1 in Act 3
- Never leave a planted loop unanswered
- Never plant a loop you can't answer

## LANGUAGE RULES

### Banned Vocabulary (4 Tiers)

**Tier 1 — NEVER:**
delve, tapestry, nuanced, multifaceted, intricate, poignant, rich (when meaning "detailed"), profound (as filler)

**Tier 2 — RARELY (max 1-2 per script):**
transformative, journey (except literal travel), beacon, resonate, deeply (as intensifier)

**Tier 3 — CAUTION (only with theological precision):**
sacred, divine, holy, anointed, blessed

**Tier 4 — CONTEXT (OK with scripture citation):**
power, glory, kingdom, covenant, grace

### "You" Language
- Count 60+ "you"/"your" instances
- Every paragraph should address audience directly
- "You see this and you think..." not "One might see this and think..."

### TTS Optimization
- Write for EAR, not EYE
- Short sentences (15-25 words average)
- Natural pauses via punctuation (em-dash, ellipsis, period)
- NO brackets for TTS instructions — use punctuation instead
- NO `[PAUSE]`, `[EMPHASIS]`, `[SLOW]` — TTS reads punctuation natively

## FINAL CLEAN PASS (v2.1 Critical)

Trước khi output script:
1. Xóa TOÀN BỘ brackets `[...]`
2. Xóa internal tracking notes
3. Xóa production markers
4. Xóa word count annotations
5. Output = 100% clean prose, ready for TTS

**Before (WRONG):**
```
[OPEN LOOP PLANTED] You see Jesus walking on water. [PAUSE]
[PATTERN RESET] But here's what you don't know...
```

**After (RIGHT):**
```
You see Jesus walking on water.

But here's what you don't know...
```
