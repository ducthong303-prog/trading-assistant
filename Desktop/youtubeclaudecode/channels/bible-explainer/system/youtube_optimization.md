# YouTube Optimization System — Bible Explainer

---

## TITLE VARIANTS

### Primary (use first, A/B test against B):

**A:** `Every Time Jesus Wept: He Only Cried 3 Times (Each Means Something)`

**B:** `Jesus Wept 3 Times — The Hidden Meaning Behind Each Tear`

**C:** `The 3 Times Jesus Cried (And Why Each One Matters)`

**D:** `Why Did Jesus Weep? The 3 Tears Most People Misunderstand`

### Title Rules:
- Keep "Jesus" and "Wept/Cried" in first 5 words
- Include number (3) — listicle titles outperform
- Parenthetical subtitle adds curiosity without clutter
- Max 70 characters. Mobile truncates after ~55.

---

## THUMBNAIL A/B TESTING

Use YouTube's built-in Test & Compare (requires 3 thumbnails):

1. Upload Concepts A, B, C (from thumbnail_prompts.md)
2. Run for 7-14 days or 5,000 impressions minimum
3. Winner becomes permanent. Document winning pattern.

**If Test & Compare unavailable** (small channel): Upload Concept A. After 48h, if CTR < 5%, swap to Concept C.

```markdown
Jesus of Nazareth cried exactly three times recorded in the New Testament — and each time, the original Greek uses a completely different word. Silent tears. Loud wailing. Screaming with blood in his sweat.

This is not a story about a weak man. This is a story about a God who chose to feel everything before he fixed anything.
```

## PINNED COMMENT

```
Which of the three tears hits you the hardest?

1. The silent tears at Lazarus's tomb — when he wept WITH someone in pain
2. The loud wailing over Jerusalem — when he wept FOR people heading toward destruction
3. The screaming in Gethsemane — when he wept under the weight of what HE was about to suffer

Mine is the third. Knowing what was in that cup — and choosing to drink it anyway.

Let me know yours below. I read every comment.
```

**Why:** Pinned comment with clear options drives engagement. Easy to answer = more comments = algorithm boost.

## COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

- **First hour:** Reply to every comment. YouTube weighs early engagement heavily.
- **Day 1-3:** Heart top comments. Reply to thoughtful ones.
- **Day 7:** Post community tab: "The most common answer to 'which tear' has been ___. Here's why I think that is."
- **Monthly:** Compile best comments into a follow-up short or community post.

---

## CTR BOOST TACTICS

1. **First frame of video = thumbnail image** (no black, no text). Consistency = trust.
2. **No intro animation.** First spoken word within 1 second of video start.
3. **First subtitle appears at 0:00:00.5** — autoplay viewers see it immediately.
4. **No "please like and subscribe" before the thesis lands.** Only at end card.

## PERFORMANCE REVIEW (7 Days Post-Publish)

| Metric | Check | Action |
|--------|-------|--------|
| CTR < 4% | Swap thumbnail | Try concept B or C |
| 30-sec drop >30% | Hook too slow | Re-cut opening for next video |
| Mid-video dip at Act 4 | Montage too fast | Slow next video's Act 4 by 15% |
| High engagement, low views | Thumbnail/Title problem | A/B test aggressively |
| Comments asking for more | Green light | Plan next topic in same format |
