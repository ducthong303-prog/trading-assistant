# Reusable Series Template — Bible Explainer

**Purpose:** Turn one-off videos into a scalable channel. Clone this template for every new video.

---

## VIDEO FORMATS (3 Archetypes)

### Format 1: Mystery Unveiled

**Pattern:** Surface-level story → hidden depth revealed → emotional payoff

**Examples:** "Every Time Jesus Wept," "Every Time Jesus Got Angry," "Why Jesus Asked Questions He Already Knew the Answer To"

**Structure:** Hook (hidden secret) → Setup (familiar verse) → Reveal (Greek/Hebrew) → Escalation → Thesis → Question

### Format 2: Dark-to-Hope Arc

**Pattern:** Deepest darkness → lowest point → resurrection hope

**Examples:** "The Worst Day in the Bible," "Why Good Friday Was Actually Good," "Every Time God Was Silent"

**Structure:** Descent → Bottom → Pivot → Ascent → Resolution

### Format 3: Historical Reveal

**Pattern:** Biblical event → historical/archaeological evidence → modern implication

**Examples:** "The Day the Sun Stopped (And What Astronomy Says)," "The Census That Changed Everything"

**Structure:** Event → Eyewitness → External Source → Implication

---

## REUSABLE EMOTIONAL ARC (6-Act)

```
Act 1 — MYSTERY     Slow, intimate, curiosity      ~30% of runtime
Act 2 — TENSION     Rising, contrast, stakes        ~18%
Act 3 — BREAKING    Peak intimacy, raw emotion      ~23%
Act 4 — COST        Fast, relentless, consequences  ~14%
Act 5 — PEAK        Cosmic, devastating, thesis     ~8%
Act 6 — RESOLUTION  Stillness, hope, question       ~7%
```

**Apply to any emotional theology video.** Adjust percentages slightly per topic.

## REUSABLE 8-SILENCE MAP

Every video should have 6-8 deliberate silence moments:

| Position | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| After the hook reveal | Let mystery land |
| After emotional character quote | Grief/pain breathes |
| Entering darkest section | Atmosphere shift |
| After rawest emotional detail | Viewer processes |
| After the thesis line | Let truth settle |
| After the peak | Everything stops |
| Before resolution | Transition to hope |
| After final question | Fade to silence |

## TOPIC GENERATION PROMPT

When brainstorming next video:

> "Give me 5 Bible topics that fit the Mystery Unveiled format: a surface-level verse or story most people know, but beneath the English translation there's a hidden meaning most people miss. Each topic needs: 1 Hebrew/Greek word reveal, 1 non-Bible historical source, and an emotional payoff."

## CHANNEL GROWTH LOOP

```
Video Published
  → High CTR (6%+) from proven format
  → High retention (50%+ at midpoint) from 6-act arc
  → High engagement (comments answering end question)
  → YouTube recommends to similar audience
  → New viewers see consistent format → subscribe
  → Next video: repeat with new topic
```

**Key insight:** Format consistency > topic variety. Viewers subscribe to the *experience*, not the *topic*.
